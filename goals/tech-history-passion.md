# Technology & Hardware Passion

## Description

A personal goal driven by love of technology and hardware. Collecting, reviving, and documenting weird/old computer hardware. Living in Silicon Valley surrounded by computing and AI history. Writing about the Generative AI revolution.

## Why It Matters

This is **personal fulfillment**, not primarily about income:

- **Pure joy**: The satisfaction of bringing old hardware back to life
- **Historical preservation**: Documenting technology before it's forgotten
- **Unique perspective**: Living in Silicon Valley during the AI revolution
- **Creative outlet**: Writing about what you're witnessing firsthand
- **Legacy**: The GenAI book could be a lasting contribution

## Activities

### Hardware Collection & Revival
- Collecting old/weird computer hardware
- Reviving discontinued devices:
  - Humane AI Pin (revived)
  - Original Google Glass (revived)
  - (others?)
- Documenting the revival process
- Sharing knowledge with collector community

### Silicon Valley Living
- Appreciating proximity to tech history
- Visiting museums, landmarks, historic sites
- Meeting people who shaped the industry
- Witnessing the AI revolution firsthand

### The GenAI Book
- **Working title**: "The Short But Busy History of the Generative AI Revolution"
- Documenting the rapid evolution from GPT-3 → GPT-4 → Claude → open source explosion
- First-hand perspective as a practitioner, not just observer
- Timeline: 2020-2026 (and ongoing)
